FG Tournament Manager v1.1.0 Manual
========================================

1. Overview
----------------------------------------
A tool for running fighting game tournaments, designed to be used
with a screen reader (PC-Talker / NVDA).
It supports three formats: single elimination, double elimination
and round robin league (2 to 64 players; double elimination needs
3 to 64).
When you enter a result, it can also update a viewer page that your
friends can open in a browser on their phones or PCs
(online publishing, chapter 9).
The screen and speech can be switched between English and Japanese
(chapter 5).
The Japanese manual is Manual (Japanese).txt.

2. Requirements
----------------------------------------
- Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit)
- Screen reader: PC-Talker or NVDA recommended
  (the app works without one, but nothing is read aloud)
- No installation and no administrator rights needed

3. Installing and starting
----------------------------------------
- Extract the zip to any folder
- Run FGTournament_v1.1.0.exe in the fg-tournament-1.1.0-test
  folder
- Do not put it in Program Files. The app writes its saved data in
  the same folder, so extract it somewhere writable, such as
  Documents
- If Windows shows a protection screen (SmartScreen) the first time,
  choose "More info" and then "Run anyway"
- Saved data and settings go in the data folder in the same place.
  To remove the app, just delete the whole folder
- When you update from an earlier version, copy the data folder from
  the old folder to the new one. Your saved tournaments, publish
  settings and viewer link carry over
  (if you do not copy it, the app starts fresh)

4. Creating a tournament
----------------------------------------
Press Ctrl+N. Three screens open one after another.
 (1) Tournament settings: tournament name, format, length of
     regular matches, and separate lengths for the Final (Winners
     Final), the Losers Final, the Grand Final and the Third Place
     Match. Also: Grand Final Reset (double elimination) and Third
     Place Match (single elimination)
 (2) Register players: enter one name per line (2 to 64 players;
     3 to 64 for double elimination).
     Each time you press Enter for a new line, the app reads the
     name you entered and the count (for example "Tanaka, player 1").
     Press Tab in the text box to move to the Next button
 (3) Order: F8 shuffles. Check the order by listening, then choose
     "Generate in This Order"
After the bracket is generated, every match is listed in the main
window. Empty spots left by seeding are handled automatically as
byes.
A round robin league with many players has many matches
(2016 matches for 64 players). The list can take a moment to load.

5. Main window and the four views (switch with F6)
----------------------------------------
 (1) Matches by Round: read every match with the Up and Down arrows
 (2) Player Progress: each player's wins and losses so far, and
     their next match
 (3) Next Matches: matches that can be played now, in playing order
 (4) Standings: while the tournament is running, who is still in and
     who is out; after it ends, the final standings
Press F5 at any time to hear the overall status (progress, next
match, champion).

Switching the language:
On the Language menu (Alt+L; the menu is called "Language / 言語"),
choose "English" or "日本語". The menus, the screen and speech
switch right away. The app remembers your choice and starts in the
same language next time. The very first time, it starts in Japanese
if Windows is set to Japanese, and in English otherwise.
Saved tournaments open in either language. Tournament names and
player names are shown exactly as you entered them.

6. Entering results
----------------------------------------
Select a match in the list and press Enter.
A list of options opens, such as "Tanaka 2 to 0", "Tanaka 2 to 1",
"Suzuki 2 to 1" ... "Ito wins by forfeit". Choose one with the Up and
Down arrows and press Enter.
After you confirm, the app reads something like:
"Tanaka wins, 2 to 0. Tanaka advances to Winners Final.
Ito goes to Losers Semifinal."
In double elimination, if the losers side player wins the Grand
Final, a Grand Final Reset is added automatically.
In a round robin league, when you enter the last match the app
reads "All matches are finished. (name) is the champion." (or says
that there is a tie for first place).
After a third place match, it reads who takes 3rd and 4th place.

7. Editing and undoing
----------------------------------------
- Ctrl+Z: undo the last result, or the last edit.
  The app reads the match that was undone, so you know what changed.
  Undoing an edit brings back the result from before the edit,
  together with the results of later matches that the edit cleared
- Enter on a match that already has a result: edit the result.
  If the edit changes the winner, you first see the list of later
  matches whose results will be cleared, and then confirm.
  Changing only the score does not affect other matches

8. Saving and opening
----------------------------------------
- The tournament is saved to the data folder automatically after
  every result
  (to autosave.json until you save it under a name, then to that
  file)
- When you create a new tournament, the previous autosave.json is
  kept in the data folder as autosave_backup_(date)_(time).json.
  You can open a tournament you never saved under a name from
  there with Ctrl+O
- Ctrl+S: save / Ctrl+Shift+S: save as
- Ctrl+O: open a saved tournament (you can continue where you left
  off)

9. Online publishing (optional)
----------------------------------------
This feature updates a viewer page every time you enter a result,
so your friends can follow the tournament on their own phones or
PCs.
- You need the URL of a relay server and an "admin key".
  Neither is included in the zip. The files for the server are in the
  web folder of https://github.com/Takahashi618/fg-tournament , and
  the steps are in "Setting up a server for online publishing" in the
  English README:
  https://github.com/Takahashi618/fg-tournament/blob/main/README.en.md
  If someone else runs a server, you can also get them from that
  person
- The relay URL must start with https:// (so the admin key is never
  sent unencrypted)
- Setup: Publish menu → Publish Settings → enter the relay URL and
  the admin key → check "Enable online publishing" →
  "Issue (Reissue) Viewer Link" → Save
  (the app remembers the URL and the key after you enter them once)
- Publish menu → "Copy Viewer Link" puts the link to send to your
  friends on the clipboard
- While publishing is on, the app uploads automatically after every
  result. Nothing is read aloud when an upload succeeds; you only
  hear a warning when it fails.
  Press Ctrl+U to upload at any time
- Only people who know the viewer link can see the page. To change
  the link, use "Issue (Reissue) Viewer Link" in Publish Settings
  and then Save. When you save, the app deletes the data of the old
  link from the server (if you cancel, the link does not change).
  If the data cannot be deleted, you hear a warning; in that case
  the old link keeps showing the last data that was sent
- The viewer page uses the language the app was in when it
  uploaded. If you switch the language, the page changes at the next
  upload
- If your server was set up for an earlier version, replace
  viewer.html and tournament_relay.php on your server with the ones
  from this version. With the earlier viewer.html, the buttons and
  headings stay in Japanese even when you publish in English
  (viewer.html contains no admin key, so you can simply replace it).
  The new tournament_relay.php accepts up to 2 MB instead of 512 KB,
  and it does not send the data again when nothing has changed, so
  spectators download much less. When you replace it, write in the
  admin key the same way as the first time
- Publishing also works with the earlier tournament_relay.php. A
  finished 64-player round robin league is about 300 KB, but with very
  long player names (about 20 full-width characters) it can go over the
  512 KB limit (chapter 12)

10. Using the viewer page (for friends watching)
----------------------------------------
- Just open the link you received in a browser. The page refreshes
  every 15 seconds
- Use the buttons to switch between "List view" and "Bracket view".
  The list view reads well with a screen reader
- When a result comes in, a one-line notice appears, such as
  "Update: ... wins ..."
- The standings are a table. While the tournament is running, you
  can read who is still in and who is out
- The page is in English or Japanese, following the organizer's app

11. Keyboard shortcuts
----------------------------------------
Enter        enter the result of the selected match (edit it if it
             already has one)
F5           read the overall status
F6           switch views (by round → by player → next matches →
             standings)
F1           keyboard shortcuts
Ctrl+Z       undo the last result or edit
Ctrl+N       create a new tournament
Ctrl+O       open a tournament file
Ctrl+S       save
Ctrl+Shift+S save as
Ctrl+U       upload now (when publishing is on)
F8           shuffle (in the Order dialog)
Esc          close a dialog (cancel)
Alt+L        Language menu (switch between English and 日本語)

12. Questions and troubleshooting
----------------------------------------
Q. Nothing is read aloud
A. Check that PC-Talker or NVDA is running.

Q. The app says "The admin key is not set" or "The relay URL is not
   set"
A. Follow the setup steps in chapter 9.

Q. I heard a warning that an upload failed
A. Check your internet connection and press Ctrl+U to send it again.
   The viewer page keeps showing the last data that was uploaded.

Q. The app says "The server rejected the data as too large"
A. If tournament_relay.php on your server is from an earlier version
   (limit 512 KB), replace it with the one from this version (limit
   2 MB), in the web folder on GitHub (chapter 9). Write in the admin
   key the same way as the first time. If it still happens with the new
   tournament_relay.php, check your web server's own upload size limit
   (for example nginx's client_max_body_size).

Q. In a round robin, a player who lost once is read as "Eliminated"
A. This was a bug in earlier versions. This version never reads round
   robin players as eliminated.

Q. I cannot enter "3 to 0" in a best of 3 match
A. A best of 3 is first to 2 wins, so the score is "2 to x". The list
   only offers scores that fit the length of that match.

Q. I entered a wrong result
A. Right away, press Ctrl+Z. If you notice later, select that match
   and press Enter to edit it (chapter 7).

Q. The number of matches seems large for the number of players
A. The overall status (F5) and the viewer page count only matches
   that are actually played (byes from seeding are not counted).
   In double elimination, the Grand Final Reset is added to the count
   once it is certain to be played.

Q. The app is in Japanese / I want to use it in English
A. Open the Language menu with Alt+L and choose "English"
   (or "日本語" for Japanese).

14. Version history
----------------------------------------
v1.1.0 (2026-11-11)
- English for the screen and speech. Switch between English and
  Japanese on the Language menu (the choice is kept for the next
  start)
- Added this English manual (Manual (English).txt)
- The viewer page also shows English or Japanese, following the app
- The maximum number of players is now 64 instead of 16
  (3 to 64 for double elimination)
- The relay server now accepts up to 2 MB instead of 512 KB. It does
  not send the data to the viewer page again when nothing has
  changed, so spectators download much less (when viewer.html and
  tournament_relay.php on your server are from this version; chapter 9)
- The data sent to the viewer page is smaller (about 300 KB even for
  a 64-player round robin league). Uploads of large tournaments are no
  longer cut off on slow connections. When you enter results quickly,
  an older upload can no longer arrive late and overwrite a newer one
- Player registration now tells you right away when double
  elimination has only 2 players
- Fixed: in a round robin, a player who lost once was read as
  eliminated
- In a round robin, the end of the league and the champion (or a tie
  for first place) are now announced even when the last match does not
  involve the champion
- Fixed: after the tournament ended, editing an earlier match read its
  loser as the runner-up
- Fixed: player names containing "&" were shown incorrectly in the
  result dialog
- Pressing F5 before creating or opening a tournament now tells you how
  to create or open one
